    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does the boost last?
    Standard Boost lasts for 30 days and Premium Boost lasts for 60 days. During this time, your job listing will maintain its featured position in search results.
    Can I boost multiple job listings?
    Yes, you can boost multiple job listings. Each listing requires its own boost purchase.
    Is there a money-back guarantee?
    We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the boost service. Contact support for help.
    How soon will I see results?
    Your job listing will be boosted immediately after payment. Most employers see increased applications within 24-48 hours.
    Can I pause or cancel a boost?
    Boosts cannot be paused, but you can choose not to renew when your boost period ends. For cancellations, contact support.
