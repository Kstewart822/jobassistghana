Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: May 2026

Data Protection Compliance

This Privacy Policy explains how Job Assist Ghana collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when you use our recruitment platform. It applies to candidates, employers, recruiters, and visitors.

We handle personal data in line with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), the Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772), and other applicable Ghanaian law.

1. Introduction

Job Assist Ghana is a job and recruitment facilitation platform. We help candidates create profiles, browse jobs, submit applications, and use optional paid visibility services where available. We also help employers create company accounts, post jobs, review applicants, and unlock selected applicant contact details for recruitment purposes.

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, how it may be shared, how long we keep it, and the rights available to users.

This policy applies to:

  • Candidates and job seekers
  • Employers, recruiters, and company representatives
  • Visitors to our website and public pages
  • Users of related platform services, tools, and payment flows

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Candidate Account and Profile Information

When candidates create accounts, complete profiles, apply for jobs, or use optional paid services, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • NSS number or SHS graduate status
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Gender
  • Date of birth (optional)
  • Ghana Card number
  • Digital address
  • Region
  • Field of study
  • Experience level
  • Highest level of education
  • Preferred industry
  • Skills, currently limited in the platform form
  • Disability status
  • Password and login credentials
  • Optional profile picture, if you choose to upload one

Candidates no longer upload general supporting documents during signup. Supporting files are attached per job application instead.

2.2 Employer and Company Information

When employers or recruiters create accounts or manage company profiles, we may collect:

  • Company name
  • Industry or field
  • Business type
  • Company size
  • Business registration number (optional)
  • Company description
  • Recruiter or contact person’s full name
  • Position or job title
  • Work email where provided
  • Company address
  • City and region
  • Phone number used for login
  • Password and login credentials
  • Optional social media links, currently limited in the platform form
  • Job postings and recruitment activity

Employer social media links are collected for account or company context only. They should not be assumed to be publicly displayed on every job card or job detail page.

2.3 Job Application Documents

Candidates may upload documents for a specific job application. These may include CVs, resumes, certificates, cover letters, or image files supported by the platform at the time of upload. These files are attached to that application record rather than to the candidate signup form generally.

2.4 Payment Information

When a user pays for a platform service, we may store relevant payment records such as:

  • Service paid for
  • Amount
  • Date
  • Reference or transaction ID
  • Payment status

Payments may be processed through secure third-party providers such as Paystack, where applicable. We do not claim to store your debit or credit card details.

2.5 Automatically Collected Technical and Usage Data

When you access the Platform, we may automatically collect:

Information Type Examples
Device Information Desktop, mobile, browser type
Technical Information IP address, operating system
Usage Data Pages viewed, actions taken, searches, and time spent on the Platform
Activity Logs Login times, notifications, payment status activity, and security logs
Cookies and Tracking Cookies and tracking data

3. How We Use Information

We use information we collect to:

  • Create and manage accounts
  • Support profile completion and profile management
  • Process and store job applications
  • Allow employers to review applicants for their posted jobs
  • Enable employer applicant unlocks where available
  • Process payments for optional candidate services and employer services
  • Display employer job postings and company information to candidates
  • Send notifications and platform updates
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, misuse, or security incidents
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and support obligations

4. Sharing of Information

4.1 Sharing with Employers and Candidates

The platform is designed to connect candidates and employers, so some information may be shared as part of that functionality:

  • Candidate profile and application details may be shared with employers when a candidate applies or where platform functionality allows.
  • Candidate contact details and attached application documents may be available to employers when an application is submitted and/or when contact details are unlocked, where applicable.
  • Employer company and job posting details may be shown to candidates who browse or view jobs.

4.2 Sharing with Service Providers

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the platform, such as payment processors, hosting providers, analytics or technical support providers, and security or communications tools.

4.3 Legal Disclosures

Legal Disclosures

We may disclose information where required by law, court order, lawful regulatory request, or where reasonably necessary to:

  • Protect users or the platform
  • Investigate abuse, fraud, or security threats
  • Enforce our rules or respond to disputes

5. Application Documents

Candidates upload supporting files per job application, not as a general signup requirement. Candidates should ensure they attach the correct document for each application.

  • Application documents are linked to the specific job application where they were uploaded.
  • Employers should use those documents only for recruitment review and assessment.
  • Where an employer can view application documents, that access should be limited to the relevant recruitment context.

6. Payments

Candidate paid services and employer paid services may be processed through secure third-party providers such as Paystack, where applicable.

  • We do not claim to store your card details.
  • We may store transaction references, payment status, amount, date, and service type for support, reconciliation, and compliance purposes.
  • Payment records may be used to activate services, investigate failed transactions, and respond to refund requests.

7. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep users signed in where appropriate
  • Remember preferences and improve usability
  • Measure usage and troubleshoot performance issues
  • Support fraud prevention and platform security

You may control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some features.

8. Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for platform operations, support, compliance, and dispute handling.

Information Type General Retention Approach
Active account and profile data While the account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward for support, compliance, and dispute management
Job applications and application documents For as long as needed for recruitment workflows, records, legitimate business purposes, and legal compliance
Payment and transaction records As reasonably necessary for accounting, audit, support, and legal obligations
Security, fraud, and system logs For as long as reasonably needed to maintain security, investigate abuse, and comply with applicable law

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal data, including access controls, secure hosting practices, monitoring, and other measures appropriate to the service.

  • Access controls and role-based restrictions
  • Reasonable protection for account credentials and records
  • Security monitoring and technical troubleshooting
  • Processes for handling abuse, fraud, or suspicious activity

Important

No internet-based platform is completely risk free. Users should protect their passwords, devices, and account access details.

10. User Rights under Ghana Data Protection Act

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion where appropriate
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to or restrict certain processing in appropriate circumstances
  • Raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details in Section 15.

11. International Data Transfers

Some services or infrastructure used by the platform may involve storage or processing outside Ghana. Where that happens, we aim to use appropriate safeguards consistent with reasonable security practices and applicable data protection requirements.

12. Children and Underage Users

Underage Use

The platform is intended for users who are legally able to use recruitment services in accordance with applicable law. If we become aware that personal data has been provided inappropriately by an underage user, we may restrict the account or remove the relevant information.

13. Third-party Links

Important

The platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of third parties, and you should review their own policies where relevant.

14. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform changes, legal requirements, or operational updates. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
